P-01 Field failure traced to one drilling stationCLOSED · CAPA
100%
BURR REMOVAL AT SOURCE
Problem
A compressor came back from the customer with metal burrs inside it — a field failure on finished product already with the customer.
Difficulty
The block ran on a single transfer machine with nine sequential workstations — grinding, milling, facing, drilling — any of which could have produced the burrs, and nothing in the process data pointed at one of them.
Method
Teardown failure analysis on the returned unit, a Cp/Cpk study across all nine stations, then a week of production monitoring standing at the shortlisted machines. Burr formation isolated to the final drilling station.
Countermeasure
A needle-type air purge across all four leg bores, so a part cannot leave the station with burrs present — error-proofing at source rather than inspection downstream.
