Problem Solving

Field failure traced to one drilling station

8DCp/CpkGembaPoka-YokeCAPASamsung
Problem
A compressor came back from the customer with metal burrs inside it — a field failure on finished product already with the customer.
Difficulty
The block ran on a single transfer machine with nine sequential workstations — grinding, milling, facing, drilling — any of which could have produced the burrs, and nothing in the process data pointed at one of them.
Method
Teardown failure analysis on the returned unit, a process capability (Cpk) study across all nine stations to find which was drifting, then a one-week production monitoring study standing at the shortlisted machines. Burr formation isolated to the final drilling station on the leg portion of the block.
Countermeasure
Engineered a needle-type air purge attachment targeting all four leg bores, so a part physically cannot leave the station with burrs present — error-proofing at source rather than added inspection downstream.
Result
Internal burr accumulation eliminated and downstream rework removed entirely. Closed as a verified CAPA, then rolled out to the other stations running the same operation.

Line ergonomics & workstation improvement

REBAErgonomicsTime StudyNoise SurveySamsung
Problem
Operators at high-frequency stations were carrying avoidable physical load, and plant noise had never been mapped against the layout.
Method
Scored the workstations with REBA, then specified screw-gun balancers and stator lifters at the stations carrying the most load. Separately surveyed noise plant-wide and matched it to the layout.
Result
REBA scores improved at the treated stations, and enclosures were specified at the highest-dB areas — approved and installed.

Lean & Improvement

Tool localization & cost reduction

Supplier EvaluationToolingCost ReductionSamsung
Problem
Consumable tooling on the compressor line was driving both cost per part and changeover downtime — abrasive film at the shaft super-finishing stage, and drill bits wearing out faster than the schedule allowed.
Method
Sourced and evaluated a higher-yield abrasive film roll for the super-finishing stage, holding the same surface quality while getting more output per roll. Separately evaluated drill bit coating alternatives to extend tool life.
Result
More output per roll and less changeover downtime at the super-finishing stage, with hole quality and dimensional accuracy held through the drill bit change.
